# Future features

Ideas for what the app could do next. Nothing here is built yet. When one ships, move it out of this list
(and update [`FEATURE-PARITY.md`](FEATURE-PARITY.md) if it changes how the app compares with Fitbit and Strava).

## Requested

- **Active map tracking, like Strava.** While recording, a live map that follows you: your position, the line
  drawn so far, current pace, distance, time and splits, with the screen-locked recording already in place
  feeding it. Later: audio cues each mile, auto-pause when you stop, and a route to follow.
- **Term reports by email.** People choose a term length (daily, weekly, monthly, a training block, or the
  length of their plan) and get a PDF report emailed at the end of each term. The PDF report already exists
  (Progress → Download a report); this needs an email sender on a domain the app owns and a scheduled job.

## Social

- **Friends in the feed.** Friends' finished activities in your Feed, with only what they choose to share.
- **Kudos and comments** on activities in the feed.
- **Challenges:** monthly distance or workout goals, solo or with friends.
- **Clubs or groups** with their own leaderboards.
- **Privacy zones** that hide the start and end of shared routes (needed before routes are ever shared).

## Tracking

- **Apple Health and Health Connect sync:** steps, sleep, heart rate and workouts without typing.
- **FIT file import** from Garmin and other watches.
- **Heart-rate zones** per activity, from a watch or a Bluetooth strap.
- **Segments:** timed stretches of road or trail with personal bests.
- **Route planner:** draw a route, see its distance and climb, then follow it while recording.
- **Rest timer** between lifting sets, with a sound or vibration.
- **Plate calculator** and warm-up sets for lifting.
- **Body measurements and progress photos**, kept on the device by default.

## Insights

- **Steps and sleep in calorie burn** and a daily readiness score.
- **Race time predictions** from recent runs.
- **Weekly coaching notes:** plain-language tips from your own data (for example, "long run grew 20% this
  week, ease off next week").
- **Goal tracking** for weight, distance and lifts, with a projected date.

## Data and privacy

- **Restore from a JSON export**, with a preview and a merge rule.
- **Optional end-to-end encryption** of the account copy.
- **Home-screen widgets** for today's plan and calories.
